# ADMIN USER GUIDE — Pillaiyarappa South Indian Corner

## 1. Accessing the Admin Portal

Navigate to `/admin` in your browser.

- **Email:** `admin@pillaiyarappa.com`
- **Password:** `Pillaiyarappa@2026`

## 2. Managing Menu Availability

- Under **Menu & Availability**, click **"Available" / "Sold Out Today"** next to any dish.
- The public menu updates immediately without requiring a redeployment or server restart.

## 3. Catering Enquiries & Export

- Incoming leads appear in **Enquiries Inbox**.
- Click **"Call"** or **"WhatsApp"** for instant 1-tap communication with the customer.
- Change status to `contacted`, `confirmed`, or `completed`.
- Click **"Download CSV"** to export all enquiry data for spreadsheet analysis.

## 4. Updating Settings & Ordering Links

- Under **Site Settings**, enter your confirmed Zomato and Swiggy store listing URLs.
- Update delivery minimums or announcement bar notices as seasonal promotions change.
